Week 5 research consolidated **CARIVIX AI's competitor, domain, use-case, enterprise AI, product, and customer research** into a unified product and market direction.

## 6. Product, Customer & Positioning Framework

### Product Requirements Chain

```text
Data → Conversation → Analytics → Prediction → Risk → GIS → Knowledge → Evidence → Security → Decision Support
```

### Proposed Architecture

```text
Data & Integration
    ↓
AI & Analytics
    ↓
NLP / LLM
    ↓
Knowledge / RAG
    ↓
GIS / Spatial Intelligence
    ↓
Predictive / Risk
    ↓
Agent / Orchestration
    ↓
Decision Intelligence
    ↓
User Output
```

### Key Dependency Chains

```text
Data Quality → Analytics → Risk → Recommendation

Document Ingestion → Retrieval → Grounding → Evidence → Knowledge-to-Decision

Core Intelligence → Recommendation → Task Agents → Multi-Agent → Autonomous Workflows
```

### Recommended Positioning

| **Area** | **Research Conclusion** |
|------------|----------------------------|
| Product Category | AI-Powered Decision Intelligence Platform |
| Primary Problem | Fragmented information and disconnected intelligence |
| Primary Market | Enterprise and Government |
| Interaction Model | Conversational Decision Intelligence |
| Trust Model | Evidence, Attribution, Traceability, and Governance |
| Differentiation | Integrated intelligence, not individual features |
| MVP Principle | Demonstrate meaningful, end-to-end decision support |
| Long-Term Direction | Proactive, agent-assisted, and eventually autonomous intelligence |
