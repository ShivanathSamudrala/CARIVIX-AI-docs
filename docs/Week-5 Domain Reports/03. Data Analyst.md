## 1. Overview

The Data Analyst Team progressed the CARIVIX AI analytics work through analytical dataset preparation, AI/ML dataset validation, KPI and derived-indicator validation, economic and correlation analysis, regional and geographic comparisons, Data Dictionary updates, and Executive Intelligence Dashboard development. The team finalized six AI/ML-ready analytical datasets containing **588,752 analytical records**, completed **6/6 lineage and quality validations**, and developed and validated the Executive Intelligence Dashboard with interactive intelligence views.
