### 2.2 Government Dataset Standardization and Integration

Standardized and integrated **3 Government Intelligence datasets**:
- Infrastructure Monitoring
- Policy Impact Analysis & Budget Analysis
- Public Program Evaluation

Standardization included column-name normalization, missing-value handling, duplicate removal, year standardization, wide-to-long transformation where required, and schema alignment.

A common structure was defined, metadata fields such as **Domain and Source** were added, and all three datasets were combined into a **Unified Government Intelligence Dataset**. The merged output was validated for schema consistency, record preservation, missing values, domain distribution, and overall structure.

**Key Result:** 3/3 government datasets successfully standardized and integrated — 100% completion.

### 2.3 Data Format and Unit Standardization

### 2.4 Economic Indicators and Derived Metrics

Analyzed all **5 standardized datasets** and reviewed their available economic and analytical indicators.

The Market and Economic datasets were converted from wide to long format for time-series analysis and Power BI compatibility. Traffic, Public Program, and ArXiv datasets were already suitable for analysis without major structural conversion.

Dataset-specific derived metrics were calculated, including:

- **Market:** YoY Growth, Average, Minimum, Maximum, Standard Deviation, Latest Value and Latest Year.
- **Economic:** YoY Growth, Average, Minimum, Maximum, Standard Deviation, Latest Value and Latest Year.
- **Traffic:** Traffic Density, Congestion Index, Emission per Vehicle, Energy Consumption per Vehicle, Peak Hour Indicator and related temporal/accident metrics.
- **Public Program:** Active Job Card Rate, Worker Participation Rate, Women/SC/ST Participation Rates, Work Completion Rate, expenditure shares and Average Expenditure per Worker.
- **ArXiv:** Title Length, Abstract Length, Summary Word Count, Category Count and Primary Research Category.

Based on the metrics explicitly documented in the report, **38 derived metrics were prepared across the 5 datasets**. In addition, **5 summary datasets** were generated.

A total of **10 analytical output files** were produced: five datasets containing derived metrics and five corresponding summary datasets. Power BI interactive chart preparation was also supported using slicers, cross-filtering, and suitable visualization types.

**Key Result:** 5/5 datasets processed, 38 listed derived metrics prepared, and 10 analytical deliverables generated.

### 2.5 Cross-Source Data Consistency Validation

Validated all **5 standardized datasets** for structural and logical consistency.

The validation covered:
- Dataset loading and record verification
- Schema consistency
- Data types
- Traffic timestamp formats
- Cross-source schema comparison
- Year consistency
- Common-field datatype consistency
- Metadata consistency

A detailed comparison between the Economic and Market datasets confirmed that both datasets contain **45 years of data from 1981–2025 with no missing years between them**, resulting in **100% temporal alignment**.

The common fields were also datatype-consistent: **Year = int64** and **Value = float64** in both datasets.

Metadata analysis identified **197 unique countries in the Economic dataset and 263 reporting entities in the Market dataset**. The difference was attributed to regional groups, income classifications, aggregated reporting regions, and source-specific naming conventions rather than underlying data inconsistencies.

**Key Result:** All 5 standardized datasets successfully validated and confirmed suitable for downstream analytics, dashboards, and visualization.
