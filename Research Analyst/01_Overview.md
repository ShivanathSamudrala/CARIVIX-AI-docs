## 1. Overview

CARIVIX AI conducts structured research on competitors, market trends, domains, use cases, enterprise AI adoption, customers, and product capabilities. The purpose is to convert research findings into useful insights that support product development, strategic planning, and decision-making.

---

## 2. Research Scope

The research scope covers the following key areas:

| **#** | **Research Area** | **Purpose** |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Competitor Analysis | Understand market landscape and competitive positioning |
| 2 | Domain Research | Identify relevant business and operational intelligence problems |
| 3 | Use-Case Validation | Validate strategically relevant CARIVIX applications |
| 4 | Enterprise AI Adoption | Identify conditions influencing enterprise adoption |
| 5 | Product Requirements | Establish the capability chain needed for decision intelligence |
| 6 | Competitive Gaps | Highlight opportunities for differentiation |
| 7 | Feature Prioritization | Identify capabilities requiring highest product attention |
| 8 | Market Positioning | Position CARIVIX AI within the market |
| 9 | Customer Segmentation | Clarify primary market segments and buyer roles |
| 10 | Evidence Management | Maintain traceable research documentation |

These areas collectively help identify:

- Market opportunities
- Customer needs
- Required capabilities
- Competitive differentiation
- Product priorities for CARIVIX AI

---

## 3. Research Methodology

The research follows a structured process:

```text
Research → Analyse → Validate → Compare → Identify Gaps → 
Define Requirements → Prioritize → Recommend
```

**Process Steps:**

| **Step** | **Description** |
|----------|-----------------|
| Research | Gather information from reliable sources |
| Analyse | Analyze findings against CARIVIX AI requirements |
| Validate | Validate findings where applicable |
| Compare | Compare capabilities across competitors and domains |
| Identify Gaps | Identify competitive and product gaps |
| Define Requirements | Translate findings into product requirements |
| Prioritize | Prioritize capabilities and features |
| Recommend | Provide strategic recommendations |

Information is documented through:

- Research reports
- Comparison matrices
- Prioritization frameworks
- Supporting evidence
