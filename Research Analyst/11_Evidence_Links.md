## Purpose

This document maintains the key research documents and supporting evidence used across the CARIVIX AI Research Repository. It provides a centralized reference for competitor analysis, domain research, use cases, product requirements, feature prioritization, market positioning, and customer analysis.

---

## Source Standards

Research should prioritize the following source types:

| **#** | **Source Type** | **Description** |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Official Product Documentation | Official websites, product documentation, technical documentation, and product announcements from technology providers |
| 2 | Government and Institutional Sources | Government agencies, public institutions, regulatory bodies, and recognized international organizations |
| 3 | Research Publications | Academic papers, research studies, journals, and recognized research institutions |
| 4 | Established Industry Reports | Reports and analysis from established research and consulting organizations |
| 5 | Reputable Technology Publications | Established technology news, analysis, and industry publications with credible editorial standards |

## Evidence Usage

Major research findings should be supported by relevant evidence wherever applicable. Sources should be selected according to the source standards defined above, with preference given to official, institutional, research, established industry, and reputable technology sources.

This document serves as the centralized evidence index, while detailed analysis and findings remain within their respective research documents.
