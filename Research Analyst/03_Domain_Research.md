## 1. Overview

This document consolidates CARIVIX AI domain research across all target intelligence areas. Each entry identifies the intelligence application, the business or government problem it addresses, the detailed requirement, the required dataset and its source, key KPIs, analytical and AI requirements, the decision it supports, the potential CARIVIX capability, and its priority.

**Priority legend**

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `High` | Required for core product capability; build early. |
| `Strategic` | Longer-horizon differentiator; depends on foundational capabilities. |
| `Medium` | Valuable but deferrable. |

**ID prefixes**

| Prefix | Scope |
|---|---|
| `G##` | Government / public-sector domains (§2–§9) |
| `E##` | Economic domains (§10–§17) |
| `X##` | Cross-domain capabilities (§18) |
