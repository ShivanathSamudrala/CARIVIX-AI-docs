# Graphic Designer — Overview

## 1. Overview

The CARIVIX AI Graphic Design Team focuses on creating a **consistent, professional, and user-focused visual system** for the CARIVIX AI platform.

The team works across:

- Product UI
- Onboarding
- Feature illustrations
- Presentations
- Promotional graphics
- Brand assets

The objective is to **clearly communicate CARIVIX AI's capabilities** and maintain a **unified visual identity** across all digital and promotional assets.

---

## 3. Design Methodology

The design process follows a **structured, iterative approach**:

```
Understand → Research → Plan → Design → Refine → Validate → Standardize → Organize
```

### Process Breakdown

| Stage | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Understand** | Understand product requirements and user needs |
| **Research** | Research references, patterns, and visual direction |
| **Plan** | Plan the visual communication approach |
| **Design** | Create the required designs and illustrations |
| **Refine** | Refine according to the CARIVIX AI design system |
| **Validate** | Validate consistency and usability |
| **Standardize** | Standardize the visual output |
| **Organize** | Organize finalized assets for development, presentation, and future reuse |
