# CARIVIX AI — Marketing Assets

## 1. Overview

The CARIVIX AI Marketing Assets were designed to **strengthen the brand's digital presence** and provide **reusable promotional visuals** for website, social media, product promotion, and future marketing campaigns.

The marketing design work focused on creating a **modern, minimal, AI-focused visual language** that communicates CARIVIX AI's technology and services while maintaining consistency across different digital platforms.

### Design Emphasis

| 2 | Product communication |

| 4 | Consistent visual identity |

| 6 | Strong visual hierarchy |
| 7 | Reusable layouts |
| 8 | Platform adaptability |

> The documented marketing work specifically included **promotional graphics, a Website Hero Banner, a Social Media Banner, and advertisement creatives**.

## 9. Visual Design Language

The marketing assets followed a **consistent visual language**.

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Minimal & Modern Layout** | Compositions kept clean instead of using excessive visual elements |
| **AI-Focused Aesthetic** | AI-inspired visuals, particularly the globe illustration, communicated the technological nature of CARIVIX |
| **Soft Gradients** | Soft purple gradients used as part of the documented visual direction |
| **Abstract Geometry** | Abstract geometric background elements added depth without overwhelming the main content |
| **Floating Cards** | Rounded feature cards used to present supporting information |
| **Soft Shadows** | White cards with subtle shadows provided separation and depth |
| **Dotted Connectors** | Dotted connector lines visually connected the AI globe with feature information |

> These elements together created the documented **modern AI-focused and professional corporate appearance**.
