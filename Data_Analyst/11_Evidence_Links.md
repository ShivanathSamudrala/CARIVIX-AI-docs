## 9. Conclusion

The Data Analyst team produced a **complete evidence trail** across all four sprints, covering:

-  Data source identification and collection
-  Data quality and validation
-  KPI development and mapping
-  Analytical dataset preparation (6 datasets, 588,752 records)

-  Economic relationship and correlation analysis
-  Regional, state, and district comparison
-  Executive Intelligence Dashboard

All deliverables are **traceable**, **verifiable**, and **audit-ready** for sprint reviews and stakeholder presentations.

> **Key Principle:** Every claim in the Data Analyst documentation should be traceable to a specific piece of evidence — source document, validation report, dataset, or dashboard.
