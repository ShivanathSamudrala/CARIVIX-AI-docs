# KPI Development and Mapping

## 1. Overview

The KPI Development and Mapping work progressed across four sprints, establishing a structured framework for defining, deriving, mapping, and validating Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) across the CARIVIX AI analytical domains.

| Sprint | Focus |
|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | KPI Foundation |
| Sprint 2 | Derived Metrics |
| Sprint 3 | KPI Mapping |
| Sprint 4 | KPI Validation |

---

## 2. Sprint 1 — KPI Foundation

Sprint 1 established the initial KPI documentation requirements.

### Activities

- Identified relevant business metrics
- Identified government and economic KPIs
- Defined KPI names
- Documented descriptions
- Defined units
- Documented formulas / definitions
- Identified data sources
- Studied reporting requirements

> Sprint 1 specifically records KPI documentation for **government, economic and analytical domains**.
