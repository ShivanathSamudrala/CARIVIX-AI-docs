# Regional, State and District Comparison

## 1. Overview

The Regional, State and District Comparison work established a structured geographic analysis capability across the CARIVIX AI analytical datasets. The work progressed across four sprints, moving from initial comparison requirements to dedicated geographic comparison datasets with validated mappings.

| Sprint | Focus |
|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Initial Comparison Requirements |
| Sprint 2 | Analytical Outputs and Interactive Charts |
| Sprint 3 | Analytical Relationships and Dashboard Views |
| Sprint 4 | Dedicated Geographic Comparison Datasets |

## 9. District vs State Comparison

District-level values were compared with their corresponding **state-level values** to identify positive and negative deviations across the five comparison metrics.

> This comparison enables identification of districts that perform **above or below** their state average across Active Jobcard Rate, Worker Participation, Women Participation, Work Completion, and SC/ST Participation Total.
