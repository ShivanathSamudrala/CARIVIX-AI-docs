# Analytical Dataset Development

## 1. Dataset Development Across Sprints

The analytical dataset work progressed in stages across four sprints.

| Sprint | Focus |
|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Initial Dataset Foundation |
| Sprint 2 | Consolidation and Standardization |
| Sprint 3 | Final Analytical Dataset Preparation |
| Sprint 4 | AI/ML-Ready Dataset Preparation |

---

## 2. Sprint 1 — Initial Dataset Foundation

The team identified and collected sample datasets covering:

| # | Dataset Category |
|---|---|
| 1 | Market Analysis |
| 2 | Economic Trend Analysis |
| 3 | Public Program Evaluation |
| 4 | Traffic Analysis |
| 5 | Research Intelligence |

> **Sprint 1 recorded an initial dataset inventory of 60,079 records across these datasets.**

---

## 3. Sprint 2 — Consolidation and Standardization

The datasets were consolidated and standardized to provide consistent structures for downstream analytics.

### Activities

- Standardizing field structures
- Standardizing data types
- Standardizing applicable units
- Preparing derived metrics
- Creating analytical datasets
- Preparing datasets for dashboard and AI workflows

> The Sprint 2 documentation describes the objective as creating a **reliable standardized analytical data foundation** for downstream AI, analytics and dashboard activities.
