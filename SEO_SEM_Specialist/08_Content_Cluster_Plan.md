# CARIVIX AI — Content Cluster Plan

## 1. Overview

This document defines the **SEO content cluster architecture** for CARIVIX AI. It organizes content into **pillar and supporting pages** across 12 major topic clusters, each aligned to:

- A primary keyword
- A search intent (Commercial, Informational, or Comparison)
- A pillar/supporting content structure

> This structure builds **topical authority** by interconnecting pillar pages with their supporting content, ensuring every page has a clear role in the site's search relevance.

## 16. Content Cluster Best Practices

| # | Practice |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every supporting page should link back to its cluster's pillar page |
| 2 | Pillar pages should link out to all supporting pages in their cluster |
| 3 | Cross-cluster linking connects related topics (e.g., Predictive Analytics ↔ Economic Intelligence) |
| 4 | Each cluster should have one primary keyword theme to avoid cannibalization |
| 5 | Pillar pages should target broad commercial intent; supporting pages should target specific informational or comparison intent |
| 6 | Content production should follow the 3-month content calendar's domain sequencing (Business & Economic → Government & Smart City → Research & Conversion) |
