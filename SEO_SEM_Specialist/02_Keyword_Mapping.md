# CARIVIX AI — Keyword Mapping Planning

## 1. Overview

This document defines the **website keyword strategy** for CARIVIX AI. It maps keywords across all major website pages, organizes them by **search intent**, and establishes **primary and secondary keyword assignments** for each page.

The keyword strategy supports CARIVIX's positioning as an **AI-Powered Research, Intelligence & Decision Support Platform** and is aligned with the five intelligence domains and revenue streams.
