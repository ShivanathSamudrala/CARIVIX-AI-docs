# CARIVIX AI — SEO & SEM Technical Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

CARIVIX is an **AI-powered research, intelligence, analytics, and decision-support platform**. The SEO and SEM work is designed to build a **structured digital-growth system** that connects:

- Search visibility
- Website relevance
- Technical discoverability
- Content authority
- Paid acquisition
- Backlinks
- Marketing analytics

The SEO/SEM domain combines:

| # | Area |
|---|---|
| 1 | Keyword strategy |
| 2 | On-page SEO |
| 3 | Technical SEO |
| 4 | Metadata |
| 5 | Internal linking |
| 6 | Content clusters |
| 7 | Off-page backlink acquisition |
| 8 | Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads |
| 9 | GA4 / GSC measurement |
| 10 | Campaign tracking |
| 11 | Conversion measurement |

> **Overall Objective:** To establish an **organic and paid acquisition layer** that supports CARIVIX's five intelligence domains and its distinct revenue streams — while keeping the website **technically crawlable, semantically relevant, measurable, and conversion-oriented**.

## 6. Technical SEO Architecture

The technical SEO plan establishes a **scalable foundation** for:

- Crawlability
- Indexability
- Search-engine discovery
- Performance
- Long-term organic growth

### 6.1 Robots.txt

The `robots.txt` file should allow search engines to crawl all important content while preventing indexing of sensitive or non-public sections such as admin dashboards and APIs.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Allow crawling of all public website pages |
| 2 | Block `/admin/`, `/dashboard/`, `/login/`, `/user/`, `/api/`, `/search?`, and `/temp/` |
| 3 | Reference the XML sitemap |

**Documented sitemap reference:** `https://www.carivix.ai/sitemap.xml`

### 6.2 XML Sitemap

| Sitemap | Purpose / Included Content |

### 7.2 Image ALT-Text Requirements

| # | Requirement | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Descriptive** | Clearly describe the subject and purpose of the image |
| 2 | **Concise** | Keep ALT text brief and meaningful; ideally around 125 characters or less where practical |
| 3 | **Contextual** | Align the ALT text with the surrounding page content |
| 4 | **Keyword Relevant** | Include the primary keyword only when it naturally describes the image |
| 5 | **No Keyword Stuffing** | Do not add multiple or unrelated SEO keywords |
| 6 | **Unique** | Use unique ALT text for distinct images |
| 7 | **Accessibility First** | Write ALT text so screen-reader users can understand the image's purpose |
| 8 | **Functional Images** | Describe the action or destination when an image functions as a link or button |
| 9 | **Decorative Images** | Use empty ALT text (`alt=""`) for purely decorative images |
| 10 | **Text in Images** | Important information shown within images should also be available as HTML text |

## 11. Paid Advertising & SEM Architecture

> CARIVIX is treated as **three revenue streams sold to different buying committees** rather than one generic B2B SaaS product.
>
> The paid media plan therefore separates **audiences, funnel stages, campaign structures, messaging and measurement**.

### 11.1 Revenue Streams

| Revenue Stream | Buyer | Deal Characteristics | Paid-Media Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SaaS – Basic (₹4,999/mo)** | SME owner, individual analyst, freelance researcher | Low friction, self-serve, short evaluation | Direct-response: Google Search + retargeting |
| **SaaS – Professional (₹24,999/mo) & Enterprise (₹1,49,999/mo)** | Business Executive, Head of Strategy / Research at a mid–large company | Sales-assisted, multi-stakeholder, demo required | LinkedIn MOFU/BOFU + Google Search |
| **Government Licensing (₹25L–₹5Cr/yr)** | Government Officer, PSU decision-maker, procurement / tender committee | Relationship- and tender-driven, 6–18 month cycle, RFP-gated | LinkedIn awareness / nurture only |
| **Consulting Services** | Investor, policy body, enterprise strategy team | Custom scoping, referral- and credibility-driven | LinkedIn thought leadership + Google branded search |

> **Note:** Every cost figure, budget number, and performance target in the source SEM plan is an **assumption**.

### 11.2 Funnel Alignment

| Funnel Stage | LinkedIn Role | Google Role | Primary KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOFU (Awareness)** | Thought-leadership content, Document Ads on AI/GovTech topics | Non-brand informational Search + YouTube via PMax | CPM, content view rate, engagement rate |
| **MOFU (Consideration)** | Lead Gen Forms gated to whitepaper / ROI calculator / webinar | Non-brand commercial-intent Search | CPL, form completion rate, cost per MQL |
| **BOFU (Decision)** | Retargeting to site visitors + CRM-matched contacts | Branded Search, competitor conquesting, transactional Search | CPA, SQL conversion rate, demo-to-opportunity rate |
| **Retention / Expansion** | Customer Match audiences for tier upgrades | RLSA on existing customers | Expansion revenue per ₹1 ad spend |

### 11.3 Planning KPI Targets

| Stage | Metric | Month 1–3 | Month 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | CPM (LinkedIn) | ₹1,400–₹1,800 | ₹1,100–₹1,400 |
| TOFU | CTR (Google Search, non-brand) | ≥1.5% | ≥2.5% |
| MOFU | CPL (LinkedIn Lead Gen Form) | ≤₹3,500 SaaS / ≤₹8,000 Enterprise/Gov | ≤₹2,500 / ≤₹6,000 |
| MOFU | CPL (Google Search, non-brand) | ≤₹1,800 | ≤₹1,200 |
| BOFU | CPA (Basic/Professional trial-to-paid) | ≤₹8,000 | ≤₹5,500 or 3–4x ROAS |
| BOFU | SQL conversion rate (demo → SQL) | ≥25% | ≥35% |
| Brand | Branded Search CPC | ≤₹15 | ≤₹12 |
