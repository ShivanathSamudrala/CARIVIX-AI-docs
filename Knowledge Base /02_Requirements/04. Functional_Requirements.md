# Functional Requirements

## Executive Summary

This document defines the functional requirements of the CARIVIX AI platform. These requirements describe the core functionalities that the system must provide to support data acquisition, intelligent analysis, predictive decision-making, geospatial visualization, reporting, and user management. The objective is to ensure that the platform meets business, technical, and user requirements while providing a scalable and intelligent decision support ecosystem.

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-01 User Authentication & Authorization

The system shall provide secure user authentication and authorization using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Different user roles such as Administrator, Research Analyst, Business User, Government Officer, and System User shall have access only to authorized modules and functionalities.

---

### FR-02 User Management

The system shall allow administrators to create, update, deactivate, and manage user accounts, roles, permissions, and profile information.

---

### FR-03 Data Acquisition

The system shall collect structured and unstructured data from multiple trusted sources including government portals, business systems, APIs, public datasets, research repositories, and external services.

---

### FR-04 Data Processing

The system shall automatically clean, validate, transform, normalize, and preprocess collected data before it is used for analytics and AI processing.

---

### FR-05 Artificial Intelligence Processing

The system shall analyze processed data using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning algorithms to identify trends, patterns, anomalies, and relationships.

---

### FR-06 Natural Language Query Processing

The system shall allow users to submit queries using natural language and generate intelligent responses using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

---

### FR-07 Predictive Analytics

The system shall generate predictive insights, forecasts, and trend analysis based on historical and real-time datasets to support strategic planning.

---

### FR-08 Business Intelligence

The system shall provide business intelligence dashboards displaying KPIs, business performance metrics, market trends, and analytical reports.

---

### FR-09 Government Intelligence

The system shall support government data analysis, policy monitoring, public service analytics, and governance-related intelligence.

---

### FR-10 Economic Intelligence

The system shall analyze economic indicators, financial statistics, inflation, GDP trends, employment data, and other macroeconomic datasets.

---

### FR-11 Smart City Intelligence

The system shall support smart city monitoring through infrastructure analytics, transportation insights, environmental monitoring, and urban planning data.

---

### FR-12 Research Intelligence

The system shall assist researchers by analyzing research datasets, publications, reports, and statistical information to generate evidence-based insights.

---

### FR-13 GIS & Spatial Intelligence

The system shall visualize geographical information using interactive GIS maps, heat maps, regional analytics, and location-based intelligence.

---

### FR-14 Dashboard & Visualization

The system shall provide interactive dashboards containing charts, graphs, KPIs, maps, filters, and visual reports for different user roles.

---

### FR-15 Report Generation

The system shall automatically generate analytical reports, executive summaries, AI-generated recommendations, and downloadable reports in multiple formats.

---

### FR-16 Search & Filtering

The system shall provide advanced search, filtering, sorting, and data exploration capabilities across all datasets and reports.

---

### FR-17 Notifications & Alerts

The system shall notify users about important events, predictions, anomalies, system updates, and scheduled reports.

---

### FR-18 API Integration

The system shall expose secure REST APIs for communication with external systems, third-party applications, and integrated services.

---

### FR-19 Audit Logging

The system shall maintain detailed audit logs of user activities, system events, data modifications, and administrative actions.

---

### FR-20 System Administration

The system shall provide administrative tools for configuration management, user management, monitoring, backup, and system maintenance.
