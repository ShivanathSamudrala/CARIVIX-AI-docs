# Non-Functional Requirements

## Executive Summary

This document defines the non-functional requirements of the CARIVIX AI platform. These requirements specify the quality attributes, operational standards, and performance expectations that ensure the platform remains secure, reliable, scalable, maintainable, and user-friendly. While functional requirements describe what the system should do, non-functional requirements define how effectively the system should perform under different operating conditions.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01 Performance

- The system shall provide fast and responsive user interactions.
- User requests should be processed with minimal response time.
- AI-generated reports and dashboards should be generated efficiently.
- The platform shall support concurrent users without significant performance degradation.
- Large datasets shall be processed efficiently through optimized data pipelines.

---

### NFR-02 Availability

- The platform shall be available 24×7 with minimal downtime.
- Scheduled maintenance activities shall be communicated in advance.
- Critical services shall automatically recover after unexpected failures.
- Backup services shall be available for disaster recovery.

---

### NFR-03 Scalability

- The system shall support increasing numbers of users and datasets.
- New intelligence modules shall be integrated without affecting existing functionality.
- Infrastructure shall support horizontal and vertical scaling.
- Cloud deployment shall enable future expansion.

---

### NFR-04 Reliability

- The platform shall ensure consistent operation during normal workloads.
- Data integrity shall be maintained throughout processing.
- Failures shall be logged for troubleshooting.
- Recovery mechanisms shall minimize service interruptions.

---

### NFR-05 Security

- User authentication shall be mandatory before accessing protected resources.
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall restrict unauthorized access.
- Sensitive information shall be encrypted during storage and transmission.
- Secure communication protocols (HTTPS/TLS) shall be used.
- Audit logs shall record important system activities.

---

### NFR-06 Usability

- The user interface shall be intuitive and easy to navigate.
- Dashboards shall present information clearly through charts, maps, and reports.
- The platform shall support responsive design across desktop and laptop devices.
- Documentation shall be available for users and administrators.

---

### NFR-07 Maintainability

- The system shall follow a modular architecture.
- Source code shall follow standardized coding practices.
- Documentation shall be updated with every major release.
- Configuration files shall be easy to manage and modify.

---

### NFR-08 Portability

- The platform shall support Windows, Linux, and macOS development environments.
- Cloud deployment shall be independent of a specific cloud provider.
- Containerized deployment shall be supported where applicable.

---

### NFR-09 Compatibility

- The platform shall support modern web browsers.
- REST APIs shall support integration with external applications.
- Standard data exchange formats such as JSON and CSV shall be supported.
- Database compatibility shall be maintained across supported versions.

---

### NFR-10 Data Integrity

- Data validation shall be performed before processing.
- Duplicate and inconsistent records shall be identified.
- Database transactions shall preserve consistency.
- Backup and restoration mechanisms shall protect data integrity.

---

### NFR-11 Logging & Monitoring

- System activities shall be logged for auditing purposes.
- Application errors shall be recorded with detailed information.
- Performance metrics shall be continuously monitored.
- Alerts shall be generated for critical failures.

---

### NFR-12 Backup & Recovery

- Regular database backups shall be performed.
- Backup schedules shall be configurable.
- Recovery procedures shall minimize data loss.
- Disaster recovery plans shall be documented.

---

### NFR-13 Documentation

- Technical documentation shall be maintained throughout the project lifecycle.
- User Manuals, Administrator Guides, API Documentation, and Developer Guides shall be updated regularly.
- Version history shall be maintained for all documentation.
- Documentation shall follow standardized templates and naming conventions.

---

### NFR-14 Compliance

- The platform shall follow organizational security policies.
- Software development shall follow coding standards and best practices.
- Documentation shall comply with project documentation standards.
- User privacy and confidential information shall be protected.

---

### NFR-15 Extensibility

- The architecture shall support future intelligence modules.
- AI models shall be replaceable or upgradeable with minimal changes.
- Additional APIs and external services shall be integrated without major redesign.
- Future technology upgrades shall be supported through modular design.

---

## Quality Attributes

- High Performance
- High Availability
- Reliability
- Scalability
- Security
- Maintainability
- Portability
- Compatibility
- Usability
- Data Integrity
- Extensibility
- Interoperability
